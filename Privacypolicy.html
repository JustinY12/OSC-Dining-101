Privacy Policy

At OSC Dining 101, we respect and protect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you provide when booking a reservation.

Information We Collect

When you make a reservation, we collect your:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Preferred booking date and time
  • Number of guests
  • Any dietary requirements

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Confirm and manage your booking
  • Contact you about your reservation if needed
  • Ensure we meet any dietary needs you share

Data Protection

Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to the OSC Dining 101 team responsible for managing reservations. We do not share your personal information with any third parties.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about your privacy or data, please contact us at:

Email: reservations@ormiston.school.nz
Phone: +64 9 535 2620
