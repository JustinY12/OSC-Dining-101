About Us

Each term, OSC hospitality students turn the school cafeteria into a fully functioning restaurant. Guests are treated to a three-course meal, thoughtfully designed and prepared by students based on a new theme each term. From cooking to serving, the students take charge of every part of the experience, gaining valuable, hands-on training in a real-world setting. Expect a welcoming atmosphere, tasty food, and a great way to support student learning. Since this is a learning environment, meals may take a little longer to arrive, but the quality ranges from hearty, home-style fare to restaurant-level cuisine.

Giving Back

There are thousands of Kiwi kids missing the start of school because their families can't afford the essentials like food, uniforms, shoes, and stationery. Many others arrive too hungry to learn. We are helping 1,100 schools and early childhood centers with food, jackets, and shoes, but there are more kids who need our help. We need your help in order to provide an additional 1,000 children with the necessities so they can start school ready to succeed. Every dollar of profit goes to KidsCan!
